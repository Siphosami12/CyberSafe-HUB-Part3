Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about cybersecurity and staying safe online.

1. What is cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, mobile devices, networks, accounts and personal information from cyber threats and unauthorised access.

2. Why is cybersecurity important?

Cybersecurity helps protect personal information, online accounts, financial information and digital devices from cyber threats.

3. What is phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack where criminals attempt to trick people into providing sensitive information through fake emails, messages or websites.

4. How can I create a strong password?

Use a long and unique password. Avoid using easily guessed information such as your name, birthday or simple number combinations.

5. What should I do if I receive a suspicious email?

Do not click suspicious links or download unknown attachments. Verify the sender and report the message if necessary.

6. What is malware?

Malware is malicious software designed to damage devices, steal information or gain unauthorised access to systems.

7. How can I protect my personal information online?

Use strong passwords, enable multi-factor authentication, keep your software updated and avoid suspicious links. Be careful about the personal information you share online.

8. What should I do if I experience a cyber incident?

Secure your affected accounts and devices, change compromised passwords and report the incident to the appropriate organisation.
